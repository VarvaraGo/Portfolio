Drawing Izmir Transport Map: Design Workshop

2023 / Workshop creator and performer

About

Having moved to Izmir 20 months ago, I noticed that the transport map of the city reflects the interaction with public transport in an unpredictable way. I started to study this topic and draw my own sketches of transport maps.

Community creation

  • At the same time, I met designers from EPAM in Turkey. We found here a common desire to create and develop a design community.
  • The community was named "Purrfect Design".
  • My workshop was the third community event.

Studied

  • theory and examples of other cities, how designers solve navigation problems in transport schemes.
  • visual language of Turkish navigation on the example of official maps and amateurs.

Collected

  • the list of misunderstandings when using the Izmir map by surrounding people.
  • the best examples of cases to solve such problems.

Actions

Before the Workshop

  • highlighted the key theoretical information.
  • divided it into stages so that it formed a step-by-step format of the workshop.
  • added examples to each section.

At the workshop

  • with the help of the community, gathered designers, developers, and external performers interested in this topic.
  • helped them analyse the current map, identify the problems they want to solve, and present their solution.

Results

3 hours

  • 3 versions of Izmir transport maps.
  • Attracting a new audience to the young Turkish community 'Purrfect Design'.
  • A cozy Friday evening in a circle of like-minded people.
